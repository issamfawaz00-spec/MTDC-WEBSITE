# Product photos

Put approved product photos here and reference them from `assets/js/products-data.js`
(e.g. `image: "assets/img/products/brand-product-500ml.jpg"`).

- Square (1:1), at least 800 × 800 px
- Plain white or light background, product centred
- JPG or WebP, ideally under 200 KB each
- File names: lowercase, dashes, no spaces

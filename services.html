Our services

Everything it takes to move products to market.

Six connected capabilities that take products from the warehouse to the buyers who reach consumers across Abuja / FCT.

Wholesale Supermarket supply HORECA supply Warehousing Logistics Brand development

Wholesale

Trade supply for the businesses that move volume through Abuja's general trade: wholesalers, retailers and open-market traders.

  • Supply in trade quantities for onward sale
  • Serving wholesalers, neighbourhood retailers and market traders
  • A single point of contact for the brands we carry

Supermarket supply

Modern retail needs dependable replenishment and well-presented shelves. We supply supermarkets with the brands they want to stock.

  • Regular replenishment to agreed schedules
  • Support with product listing and shelf presence
  • Clear communication on stock and deliveries

HORECA supply

Hotels, restaurants and caterers need the right products, delivered when the kitchen and the event need them.

  • Supply for hotels, restaurants and caterers
  • Deliveries planned around service and event schedules
  • Institutional buyers supplied through the same channel

Warehousing

Holding stock in Abuja keeps products close to customers and ready to move when orders come in.

  • Local storage for the brands we distribute
  • Stock handled with care and organised for dispatch
  • Visibility for brand partners on what is in stock

Logistics

Getting products from our warehouse to trade customers across Abuja / FCT, reliably and in good condition.

  • Deliveries to trade and institutional customers in Abuja / FCT
  • Route planning that keeps service consistent
  • Products delivered in saleable condition

Brand development

Distribution gets products into outlets. Brand development helps them sell through. We work with brand owners to build visibility and demand in the market.

  • Market entry planning for new brands in Abuja
  • In-outlet visibility and trade engagement
  • Market feedback shared with brand partners

Supermarkets · Wholesalers · Retailers · Open markets · Hotels · Restaurants · Caterers · Institutional buyers
